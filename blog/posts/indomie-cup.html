Indomie as a cup of noodles (a review)

I took this image with my iPhone SE 2nd gen

Today I consumed this cup of noodles, except it is Indomie. Usually noodle cups tend to be low budget, which correlates well with low quality. This was not the case. You are still getting the 10/10 Indomie experience here. Same noodles, same seasoning, oils, all that.

This cup does not have a fill-line on the inside, making me believe that Indomie is the true thinker and tinkerer's noodle. Perfect for when you need to apply brain powers and max out your experience when you write LaTeX papers or are deep dived in XMPP configurations. I used a microwave at my internship to heat up the water, and then I proceeded to drain the water. I mixed in the seasoning, oil, sambal, and kecap. What I got was the classic Indomie experience, except in a special Indomie cup.

Not a moment was spared as I ate the noodles. Definitely helps to have some Indomie, when you are in a pinch with no vessel to contain the Indomie in.
