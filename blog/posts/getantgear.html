Support for Autism Nature Trail! Get ANT gear now!

I had an awesome time at the Autism Nature Trail (ANT).

The ANT at Letchworth State Park is a first-of-its-kind, one-mile loop designed specifically for neurodivergent individuals and their families. It is next to the Humphrey Nature Center, the trail features interactive stations like the Music Circle, the Design/building Zone, and Cacoons to chill out in.

It takes a community to keep this incredible space clean, staffed, and fully accessible for everyone who needs it.

You can support the loop's ongoing mission directly by picking up official gear. Every purchase funds essential maintenance and inclusive programming.

Get your ANT gear now at GetAntGear.com!
